# Architecture

How the Agentic Body is put together. Five chapters, each mirrored from its
canonical document in shesh-ecosystem (`docs/architecture/`):

  and why it is shaped that way.
- [Repo Topology](architecture/repo-topology.md) — the federated-sinkhole
  layout: one manifest, many small repos.
- [Language Policy](architecture/language-policy.md) — Rust, Python, Lua,
  QML/JS, Bash only (ADR-0001), and how that is enforced.
- [Multi-Agent](architecture/multi-agent.md) — roles, budgets, and how
  agents talk.
- [ACP & A2A Protocols](architecture/acp-a2a.md) — editor/agent and
  agent/agent protocols.

Load-bearing upstream decisions live in [Part V — ADRs](../adr/README.md);
